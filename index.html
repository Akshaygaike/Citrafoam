Questions

Frequently Asked

Is it safe on all bathroom surfaces? +
Yes — safe on ceramic tiles, chrome taps, stainless steel, porcelain, and glass. Avoid natural stone (marble, granite) without testing a small patch first.
How often should I use it? +
For maintenance, once a week is ideal. For heavy limescale, use daily for the first week then switch to weekly maintenance.
Is it child and pet safe? +
When used and rinsed as directed, yes. Citric acid is a food-grade ingredient. Always rinse surfaces thoroughly and store out of reach of children.
What's the shelf life? +
24 months from manufacture when stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Manufacture date is on the bottle base.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Will it scratch my copper vessels? +
No — completely abrasive-free. It uses liquid chemistry to dissolve tarnish, not friction. Safe on all copper, brass, and bronze surfaces including heirlooms and antiques.
Can I use it on pooja room items? +
Absolutely. Safe for diyas, idols, bells, kalash, and all pooja brass/copper items. Rinse thoroughly with water after cleaning.
Does it work on stainless steel? +
Tarnish Eliminator is optimised for copper, brass, and bronze. For stainless steel and chrome, use our Mineral Dissolver No. 01 which is formulated for those surfaces.
How long does shine last after cleaning? +
Our protective polymer slows re-tarnishing. In typical conditions, shine lasts 3–6 weeks. In humid monsoon environments, clean every 2–3 weeks.
